The result is a credit early warning: companies whose own words turn worse than their last report are downgraded far more often, months later.

The platform

Read every filing as a connected system

Parse

Every 10-K, 10-Q, 20-F and 40-F split into sections; earnings calls into prepared remarks and Q&A; financial statements from XBRL as first filed.

Compare

Every reading measured against the company's industry and its named competitors in the same quarter, and against its own last filing.

Read three ways

A finance word list, FinBERT and a large language model, plus a measurement reading that counts credit-relevant disclosures with verified quotes.

Control

Ratings, outlooks and watch status; distance to default, Altman Z, Ohlson O; 23 volatility measures; 13 macro series; published text-change measures.

Prove

Pre-registered, single-run, out-of-sample tests with corrections for many tests, permutation checks and memory tests.

Record

Post-cutoff filings scored and locked with SHA-256 fingerprints before outcomes exist, every quarter.

Who it is for

One reading, three uses

Credit risk

A quarterly watch list of names whose filings turned worse than their last one, with the passages behind each flag. Strongest where the agency is still calm.

Equity research

Triage across hundreds of filings a week, including the many companies few analysts cover, ranked by how much each changed against its peers.

Wealth management

A credit early-warning light beside each holding and a plain-language reason taken from the company's own words.

How to use it

A short list, not a new rating

The readings concentrate future downgrades in a short list of names and are strongest where the agency is still calm. They are an input to a credit team's watch list, alongside its rating models, not a replacement for them. How to read the results.

Where it can go

More coverage, earlier signals, a locked record

Every quarter, new filings are scored and locked before outcomes exist (first readout March 2027). Next: broad US and Canadian coverage, credit-market outcomes, and alternative data, from app ratings to government contracts, that moves before companies report. Roadmap.
