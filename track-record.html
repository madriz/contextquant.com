Track record

Scored first, checked later

The cleanest test of any AI reading is on documents written after the model's training ended: it cannot remember outcomes it has never seen. Those filings are scored, the scores locked with a SHA-256 fingerprint, and the fingerprint recorded before any outcome exists.

Each quarter from now

A growing post-cutoff record

Every new filing is scored in the quarter it appears, the scores are locked and fingerprinted, and outcomes are added as they arrive. Credit outcomes take time: at today's downgrade rates, about 30 post-cutoff downgrades, enough for a first credit readout, are expected by late 2027.

Results

Published either way

Readouts are published whatever they show, with the test written down in advance. If the readings do not hold on filings the model cannot have seen, this page will say so.
